The person and the work

A song, a political joke or a proposal for Amity might be someone's first encounter with this collection. Open Record: Luke Nathan Hayes brings the person and the connected body of work into one account. It is a major source for this website, with routes into autobiographical records, project repositories and original documents.

The life behind the projects includes retail and practical trades, railway and airport work, festivals, travel, self-taught web design and music. The life record follows education, jobs, places lived, volunteering, relationships and personal ambitions. Those experiences give the proposals their human setting.

How the ideas developed

The development history follows earlier civic and learning ideas into the present collection. It connects the 2011-2012 reading of legislation and fine print with the 2014 Constitutional Matrix of Participation, the 2021 beginnings of 500 Queens at Festival Towers, and later work on personal intelligence, care and community capability.

The document archive supplies the papers themselves. Earlier editions, public submissions and later explanations let a reader follow how an idea changed, where it reappeared and which parts became websites or software.

Local life, wider connections

Minjerribah, North Stradbroke Island and Straddie refer to the same island. Its projects have their own island categories, covering culture and media, funding, co-operatives, places, care, ecology and other local work.

The 82 Claytons Road account connects the care partnership proposal with the newer Aura Genesis Protocol, its Truth Engine and chamber development. The Ready SET Co-op Cultural Intelligence Node gives the missing middle of computing a local setting. The wider record follows the connections into P4A, UNGA81, music and international co-operation.
