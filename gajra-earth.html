GAJRA Earth

GAJRA Earth: a garland with somewhere to begin

The Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth, proposed by Luke Nathan Hayes.

GAJRA Earth is the proposed Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth, put forward by Luke Nathan Hayes. Its purpose is to bring people and locally rooted associations together around that shared ambition. A gajra is a flower garland. Its flowers remain distinct through being connected. The metaphor carries a practical preference for locally rooted associations, each shaped by its participants, laws and culture, exchanging knowledge and undertaking work together.24

Brisbane is the proposed beginning. A founding group would give the invitation people able to carry responsibilities. A prominent home would give meetings, creative work and public inquiry a place. Funding would support specific activity and the ongoing costs of an organisation. The relationship with Hayes's intellectual property, the membership and the governing rules belong in that formation.

The inquiry itself has plenty to begin with. What does joy mean across generations? Which responsibilities have become invisible? What forms of abundance create freedom, and which transfer a burden elsewhere? A meal, a workshop or a film gives those questions experience to work with. Someone who would never join a formal debate may have a detailed answer through the way they care for a place or another person.

Locally rooted associations also create opportunities to disagree productively. One place may choose a cooperative enterprise. Another may begin with language, education or ecological work. A community may prefer different words for the purpose. The shared intelligence includes those interpretations and what happens when people try something. International connection gains meaning through the useful relationships participants actually develop.

Human self-alignment and AI alignment meet in this growing map. People describe what matters, examine the distance between intention and conduct, imagine other possibilities and bring experience back into their next choices. Selected information travels under agreed permissions. Differences remain available to understand rather than being averaged into a compulsory global personality.

A story gives an idea characters and consequences. A game lets someone make choices inside a possible world. A song carries meaning between people who do not share the same technical vocabulary. Translation also discovers things a source language failed to say. A more capable civilisation has reason to spend time on all of them.

Past, Present and Future: July 2035

July 2035 offers a gathering point around the fiftieth anniversary of Live Aid. The Past, Present and Future themes give three days different work to do. The past brings inheritance, gratitude, grief and what people choose to carry forward. The present brings the work already under way and the lives people are actually living. The future opens the possibilities people want to pursue together.24

The years before then contain the substance. Local events, care, experiments, travelling artists, shared businesses, exchanges, useful failures and friendships become the material of a gathering. Globally synchronised art and music gain depth when participants have something of their own to bring into the connection. The audience also becomes part of the inquiry through reflection and collective choices about what matters.

A big public moment would speed some work and make other parts more demanding. Interest brings invitations, offers and questions. Each consumes attention. A founder undertaking Genesis during that attention still has a finite day. Shared responsibilities let other people develop their parts without waiting for every decision to return to one person.

The formation of institutions is therefore part of the abundance being sought. Maintainers, organisers, teachers, researchers and members become authors of the continuing work. Its future gains more centres. Hayes has his own life to live within that network, including music, writing, travel and relationships. Others bring lives that deserve the same room.
